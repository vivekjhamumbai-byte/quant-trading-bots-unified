GBI-RBI - Price Action Pattern
1. RBI (Long): bar_a RED & bar_b.low > bar_a.low
2. GBI (Short): bar_a GREEN & bar_b.high < bar_a.high
3. Entry Trigger: RBI close > bar_a.high | GBI close < bar_a.low
4. Strike: RBI → CE (Long) | GBI → PE (Short)
5. Stop Level: RBI → min(bar_a.low, bar_b.low) | GBI → max(bar_a.high, bar_b.high)
